# Code of Conduct — Gap Analysis

Working checklist for finishing the code of conduct. Compares the current draft
(`code-of-conduct-google-docs.md`) against the research survey of other FTC/FRC
teams' handbooks. Organized so each item can be worked section by section.

Status key: **DONE** (in the draft) · **PARTIAL** (touched, needs more) · **MISSING** (not addressed)

## Round 3 — Fable advisor review (four lenses)

Four Fable advisors (student, family, coach, FIRST/other-teams) reviewed the post-round-2a
draft. Ranked by cross-lens agreement — an item multiple lenses hit independently is the
strongest signal. **[edited]** = implemented this pass; **[decision]** = needs Peter's call
before drafting; **[YPP-mechanical]** = unambiguous Youth-Protection additions, ready on the word.

### Tier 1 — multi-lens

1. **[decision] "Senior student" undefined but load-bearing** (student + coach). Carries authority
   (direction, dropped-gear checks, signing witness) with no way to know who counts. Proposed:
   coaches name senior students at season start; move equipment-damage checks to a coach.
2. **[decision] "Question a mentor" clause is broken** (student + coach). Sentence two swallows
   sentence one; no arbiter; collides with take-direction-argue-after. Proposed: define "question"
   as speak-then-comply-unless-safety; another coach can call a mentor "off"; link the grant in
   *Make yourself useful* to the check.
3. **[edited] Safety exception gave voice but no brake** (coach). Added: a student does not have to
   do a flagged-unsafe thing until a coach has looked.
4. **[decision] Parent-coach recusal + family recourse in discipline** (family + coach). Ladder has
   no conflict rule for a coach disciplining/shielding own child, no family appeal on a call about
   own student. Proposed: steps 3–4 need head coach + one other; parent-coach recuses; family can
   bring step 3–4 to the board.
5. **[edited] Harassment reporting path + non-retaliation** (family + coach + FIRST). Added
   report-to-any-coach/board, discretion, no retaliation, safety-risk escalation to family/
   authorities/FIRST YPP.
6. **[edited] Attendance access door** (student + family + FIRST). Kept "has not really been on the
   team"; added a line so a real conflict reads as tell-us-early — the failure mode is now the
   unseen no-show, not the honest conflict. (Closes the round-2a attendance-pointer decision.)
7. **[edited] Medical / OTC-meds** (family + coach + FIRST). Reframed "non-prescribed medication
   banned" (banned Advil) to misuse/sharing; OTC fine with parent knowledge. Emergency-contact/
   medical-form reference still **[decision]** — see gap #6 and photo-consent below.
8. **[edited] Stale "like the CNC right now"** (student + coach). Cut "right now" — dated a signed doc.
9. **[YPP-mechanical] Two-adults pickup gap** (family + coach). "Meeting ends" strands the last kids
   with one adult — the very thing forbidden. Add a pickup protocol.
10. **[edited] Tool sign-off record** (coach + FIRST; confirmed by Ben). Sign-offs now logged on a
    clipboard on the shop wall — tools as rows, students as columns, mentor initials in the cells —
    and the *Shop safety* rule says so. The five named tools are the sign-off list.
11. **[edited] Read-aloud ritual reciprocal + access** (student + family). Made it two-sided
    (coach/senior reads *What we hold ourselves to* back) and added a private/equivalent option.

## Round 2a review — core content

From the four-perspective review (students, families, coaches, FIRST/other teams) plus
voice check against the Houston post. Address these before the operational gaps below.

### Content gaps

- [x] **Senior students: authority without reciprocal duty.** *Done. Rebuilt* Respect
  everyone in the room *now carries the reciprocal clause: "that standing is a responsibility,
  not a privilege… Teach, don't boss. Explain the why when there's time."*
- [x] **No safety exception to "do the thing that was asked, argue afterward."** *Done.
  Added to* Make yourself useful*: "The exception is safety: if something looks unsafe, say
  so on the spot, before it gets done." Mirrors the mentor section.*
- [x] **Rebuild *Respect everyone in the room* to earn its title.** *Done. Now opens with a
  proactive belonging/non-discrimination paragraph (counterweight to "leave politics at the
  door"), then adults (unpaid time + parent-coaches), then student-to-student respect in both
  directions. Closes item 7 below too.*

### Naming and framing decisions

- [x] **Decide what the ten principles are called.** *Decided: NOT a creed. Stripped the
  word from* Signing and reading *— the rite now says "read the ten principles at the top of
  this page aloud" and "re-affirm them."*
- [ ] **Confirm "coaches make the call" is deliberate.** Board escalation exists only for
  concerns *about a coach*; there's no stated recourse if a family disputes a disciplinary
  call about their student. May well be the feature you want — just decide it on purpose.
- [ ] **"Standing permission to question their decisions" is fuzzy.** Who declares a
  mentor "off"? As written it only works if the mentor self-reports — which the Houston
  anecdote shows is exactly what tired people fail to do.
- [ ] **Attendance flexibility lives only in the don'ts list.** "Without prior notice or
  a real reason" implies legitimate conflicts are fine, but a multi-activity family reading
  the *Attend* section first won't find it there. Decide whether to point to it from the
  section — without softening "has not really been on the team," which stays.

### Voice

- [x] **"The standard we are trying to teach"** — a hedge. "The standard we teach" is the
  voice. (Intro, GP paragraph.) *Fixed in the Google Docs version; synced to the web version.*
- [x] **Seven-noun abstraction list in the intro** ("reliability, honesty, humility,
  discipline, focus, respect, and care…"). *Done. Now three concrete habits: "showing up
  when you said you would, owning a mistake out loud, putting the tools back before you
  leave."*
- [x] ***Notice the people around you* label-bullets** (Volume. Airtime. Presence.
  Awareness.) *Done. Reflowed into a single prose paragraph; content preserved.*
- [ ] **Principles 8 and 9 are near-synonyms read aloud** (*Respect everyone in the
  room* / *Notice the people around you*). The sections distinguish them; the one-line
  versions don't. Read-aloud test with a student. *Still open — needs an actual student.*

### Mechanical

- [x] **Woodie Flowers quote formatting.** Runs in as body text with attribution dangling
  below; needs blockquote formatting or it reads as if we wrote it. *Blockquoted in the
  Google Docs version; web version already used the quote include.*
- [x] **Hardcoded meeting times** ("Sundays 2-5, Wednesdays 5:30-7:30") force re-editing a
  signed document when the schedule shifts. *Both versions now point to the home page's
  Meeting Schedule section. Note: the index says Wednesdays 5:00–8:00 PM where the code
  of conduct said 5:30–7:30 — the index is now authoritative; confirm it's current.*
- [x] **Google Docs export artifacts** (`\-` escaped hyphens throughout) — don't let them
  leak into the Jekyll version. *Removed; prose dashes converted to em dashes matching
  the web version.*

### Confirmed — keep as-is

Not action items; guard rails against future softening. The sharp lines ("has not really
been on the team," "'Sure' beats 'why me?'"), the Houston 0-10 story, the tired-coach
anecdote, and the self-implicating "I" moments are the document's authenticity anchors.
The architecture (creed → principles → observable behaviors → hard rules → mentor
obligations → letter to families) is right; *What this looks like* is what makes the
document enforceable rather than aspirational.

## Still open — ranked by priority

### 1. DONE — PPE / shop safety
Added as the first **Hard rule** (*Shop safety*), tuned to the actual Beech Street inventory:
Bambu A1-mini, bandsaw, miter saw, drill press, Milwaukee drill, metal brake, and the CNC
(down for repair). Covers eye protection when machines run, per-tool training sign-off,
closed-toe shoes / tied hair / no loose sleeves / no dangling jewelry, one-person-at-the-
machine, unattended-print discipline (ties back to the battery-on-the-charger rule), and
tagged-out tools stay off. Phone-jail hook deliberately omitted (kept plain).

### 2. MISSING — Photo / media consent
Recommended by the research. Probably belongs as a **separate signed release**, not body
text, but the code of conduct should at least reference that such a form exists and what it
covers (team uses photos/video of students for the site, blog, social, and sponsor materials;
opt-out path for families who decline).

### 3. MISSING — Driver / leadership selection criteria
Research item 5 — the "wish we'd written this down before the season" item. Driver and
student-leadership selection criteria, written down, short-circuits politics. Deserves its
own short section and is best drafted **with senior-student input** — hold for the feedback round.

### 4. PARTIAL — PDA / relationships line
Research recommended pairing the harassment clause with a brief public-displays-of-affection
line. Currently absent. One sentence in *Hard rules → Harassment* or *Travel* (e.g., conduct
in shared/overnight spaces) would close it.

### 5. PARTIAL — External social media policy
The draft covers phones/entertainment *during meetings* well, and "respect competitors."
It does not cover students **posting about the team publicly** — e.g., not disparaging other
teams or members online, not posting shop/competition photos that identify minors without
consent. One sentence; low urgency for a small community team.

### 6. MISSING — Emergency / medical info (travel)
Not in the research list, but a real exposure for overnight travel with mixed MS/HS kids:
emergency contacts, allergies, and medical/medication info collected before trips. Partly
implied by the prescription-disclosure line under Substances; could be made explicit under
*Travel and competitions* or folded into the signing packet.

### 7. DONE — Non-discrimination / inclusion statement
Closed alongside the *Respect everyone in the room* rebuild. The section now opens: "It
doesn't matter where you go to school, what you look like, who your family is, or what you
believe outside this room. Show up, do the work, and treat the people around you well, and
you belong." Proactive counterweight to "leave politics at the door" nearby.
