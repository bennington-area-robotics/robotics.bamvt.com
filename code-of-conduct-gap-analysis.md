# Code of Conduct — Gap Analysis

Working checklist for finishing the code of conduct. Compares the current draft
(`code-of-conduct-google-docs.md`) against the research survey of other FTC/FRC
teams' handbooks. Organized so each item can be worked section by section.

Status key: **DONE** (in the draft) · **PARTIAL** (touched, needs more) · **MISSING** (not addressed)

---

## Already covered — no action needed

These were the top operational gaps the research flagged. The draft now handles them:

- **DONE — Travel & hotel conduct.** *Hard rules → Travel and competitions.* Room
  assignments (no co-ed, no non-guardian adult), buddy system, curfew, public-space
  conduct. Matches the Greenwood / FRC 1511 Rolling Thunder pattern.
- **DONE — Two-adult rule.** *Hard rules → Two adults, always.* Cites FIRST Youth
  Protection Program; includes the "meeting ends and we reconvene" enforcement.
- **DONE — Substances & weapons.** *Hard rules → Substances and weapons.* Alcohol,
  cannabis, nicotine, vape, non-prescribed meds, firearms/weapons. Adds prescription
  disclosure — a nice extra most teams omit.
- **DONE — Harassment.** *Hard rules → Harassment.* Verbal/physical/social/sexual/online,
  grounds for removal, explicitly extends to parents and coaches (the Swerve clause).
- **DONE — Disciplinary ladder.** *Hard rules → When a rule is broken.* Four-step ladder,
  severity bypass to step 3/4, parent involvement at step 2+, board escalation path for
  complaints about a coach.
- **DONE — Signed acknowledgement + reading rite.** *Hard rules → Signing and reading.*
  Student + parent signature, creed read aloud, returning-student re-affirmation.

## Still open — ranked by priority

### 1. MISSING — PPE / shop safety
The single biggest remaining operational gap. Present in nearly every handbook surveyed;
absent here. The **two-adult rule is in, but tool/PPE safety is not.**
- Eye protection required when machines are running.
- Per-tool training sign-off before a student operates power tools.
- Closed-toe shoes, tied hair, no loose sleeves near rotating equipment.
- Depends on the Beech Street shop arrangement — only as detailed as the tools you actually have.
- Optional culture hook from research: **FTC 9929 "phone jail"** — trade your phone for
  safety glasses at the door. Ties PPE compliance to the existing phone rule in
  *Robotics first, socializing second.*

### 2. MISSING — Photo / media consent
Recommended by the research. Probably belongs as a **separate signed release**, not body
text, but the code of conduct should at least reference that such a form exists and what it
covers (team uses photos/video of students for the site, blog, social, and sponsor materials;
opt-out path for families who decline).

### 3. MISSING — Driver / leadership selection criteria
Research item 5 — the "wish we'd written this down before the season" item. Driver and
student-leadership selection criteria, written down, short-circuits politics. Deserves its
own short section and is best drafted **with senior-student input** — hold for the feedback round.

### 4. PARTIAL — PDA / relationships line
Research recommended pairing the harassment clause with a brief public-displays-of-affection
line. Currently absent. One sentence in *Hard rules → Harassment* or *Travel* (e.g., conduct
in shared/overnight spaces) would close it.

### 5. PARTIAL — External social media policy
The draft covers phones/entertainment *during meetings* well, and "respect competitors."
It does not cover students **posting about the team publicly** — e.g., not disparaging other
teams or members online, not posting shop/competition photos that identify minors without
consent. One sentence; low urgency for a small community team.

### 6. MISSING — Emergency / medical info (travel)
Not in the research list, but a real exposure for overnight travel with mixed MS/HS kids:
emergency contacts, allergies, and medical/medication info collected before trips. Partly
implied by the prescription-disclosure line under Substances; could be made explicit under
*Travel and competitions* or folded into the signing packet.

### 7. MISSING — Non-discrimination / inclusion statement
Harassment covers the reactive side; many handbooks also carry a short proactive
"everyone is welcome regardless of …" line. Optional given the principle-led tone, but worth
a sentence near the top or in *Respect everyone in the room*.

## Distinctive ideas from research (optional, cultural — not gaps)

Not required for completeness, but flagged as worth considering:

- **VAHS Chief Resiliency Officer** — a student officer monitoring stress/isolation/PPE,
  escalating to mentors. Operationalizes the peer-support the draft gestures at in
  *Notice the people around you*.
- **FTC 9929 decision-authority + paper trail** — mentors record the rationale for
  design/role/sub-team decisions in the engineering notebook. Locks authority and creates
  a record; complements *Ambition needs discipline*.

---

## Suggested working order

1. **PPE / shop safety** — highest exposure, you can draft it now from the actual shop setup.
2. **PDA line + external social-media sentence** — quick, fold into existing Hard rules.
3. **Emergency/medical info** — fold into Travel or the signing packet.
4. **Photo/media consent** — decide: reference here + separate release form.
5. **Non-discrimination line** — one sentence if you want it.
6. **Driver / leadership criteria** — defer to the senior-student feedback round.
