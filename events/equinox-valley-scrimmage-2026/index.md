# Equinox Valley Scrimmage 2026

The **Equinox Valley Scrimmage** is a FIRST Tech Challenge practice event in the 2026–2027 BIOBUZZ season, hosted by Bennington Area Robotics and Equinox Valley Middle School (EVMS). Scrimmage matches do not count toward advancement: teams run full matches on a competition field to test their robots, practice driving and refereeing, and see other teams' designs before the qualifier season.

**Date:** Saturday, December 19, 2026

**Venue:** Equinox Valley Middle School, 80 Memorial Ave, Manchester Center, VT 05255

**Contact:** [info@bamvt.com](mailto:info@bamvt.com?subject=Equinox%20Valley%20Scrimmage)

## What is FTC?

FIRST Tech Challenge is a robotics program for students in grades 7–12. Teams design, build, and program robots to compete on a 12' × 12' field in alliance-based matches.
