## FIRST Tech Challenge Season Kickoff 2026

Join Bennington Area Robotics and Manchester Machine Makers to begin the 2026–2027 FIRST Tech Challenge season: **FIRST CANOPY: BIOBUZZ presented by RTX**. Teams, students, families, and community members are welcome.

**Date:** Saturday, September 12, 2026

**Time:** Starts at 11:30 AM; game reveal livestream at noon (Eastern time)

**Location:** Manchester Community Library, 138 Cemetery Avenue, Manchester Center, VT 05255

**Co-hosts:** Bennington Area Robotics and Manchester Machine Makers

### What to Expect

We will watch the game reveal livestream, then brainstorm with robots on the new game field. Attending FTC teams that RSVP will receive game elements to take home to begin working with while they await their game sets.

### Team RSVP and Event Details

FTC teams should RSVP to Manchester Machine Makers at [ftc16221@gmail.com](mailto:ftc16221@gmail.com).
