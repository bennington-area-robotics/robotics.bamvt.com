SHOP SAFETY

The shop has real tools, and real tools bite.
Safety Glasses

Safety glasses whenever a machine is running — and whenever you are within reach of someone running one. There is no "I'm just doing one cut."

Tool Training

No power tool until a coach signs you off on that tool. Bandsaw, miter saw, drill press, metal brake, and CNC each get their own sign-off, logged on the sheet on the shop wall. Knowing the drill does not mean you know the bandsaw.

Dress and Hair

Closed-toe shoes. Tie back long hair. No loose sleeves, drawstrings, lanyards, or jewelry near anything that spins or cuts.

Single Operator

Only one student operates a machine at a time, and only their hands should be near it. Other students should watch from a safe distance.

3D Prints

Don't reach into a running printer or move someone else's print. If a print will outlast the meeting, tell a coach so someone knows it's running.

Broken = off

If a tool is broken, tagged out, or mid-repair, it stays off. Don't be the one who decides it's fine. Report it if you're the first to notice.

When in doubt, stop and ask.
No one has ever been in trouble here for asking whether something is safe.
