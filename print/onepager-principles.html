Bennington Area Robotics

Code of Conduct

We teach by FIRST's standard of Gracious Professionalism.
1

Attend meetings consistently.

Show up! Working together is how a team becomes a team.

2

Show your best side.

Use clean language, show courtesy, practice humility. Leave school and politics at the door.

3

Make yourself useful.

See what needs doing and do it. "Sure" beats "why me?"

4

Mean what you say.

Let your yes be yes. An honest maybe beats a soft yes.

5

Find the cause, not the culprit.

"My part works" is a hypothesis to test. Focus on solutions, rather than pointing fingers.

6

Own your mistakes.

Name the mistake, and build new habits that prevent it.

7

Ambition needs discipline.

Big ideas need a scope, a deadline, and a fallback.

8

Respect everyone in the room.

Show up and do the work, and you belong. Teach, don't boss. Treat others as you would like them to treat you.

9

Notice the people around you.

Mind your volume and presence. Allow silence between comments. Know everyone's name.

10

Robotics first, socializing second.

Use phones for build references, not entertainment.

"Gracious professionals learn and compete like crazy, but treat one another with respect and kindness in the process." — Dr. Woodie Flowers
